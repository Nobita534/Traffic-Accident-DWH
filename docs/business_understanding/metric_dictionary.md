# Metric Dictionary

## 1. Document Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the business metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used in the Traffic Safety Analytics Platform. It provides standardized metric definitions, calculation logic, business rules, and required data to ensure consistency across analytical models, dashboards, and reports.

---

## 2. Metric Dictionary

# KPI Dictionary

| **Metric ID** | **Metric Name** | **Business Question** | **Definition** | **Formula** | **Required Data** | **Business Rules** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KPI-01** | **Total Accidents** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Total number of recorded traffic accident records within the selected filter context.<br><br>**Grain:** One fact record represents one recorded accident. | `COUNT(fact_id)` | `fact_id` | Count each accident record once using the unique `fact_id`. The denominator includes all recorded accident records, including records containing unknown, not reported, or unable-to-determine information. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-02** | **Severe Rate** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Percentage of recorded accidents classified as severe within the selected filter context. | `Severe Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `is_severe`, `fact_id` | A severe accident is defined as an accident with at least one fatal injury or incapacitating injury. `Total Accidents` follows KPI-01 and includes all recorded accident records in the selected filter context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-03** | **Fatal Rate** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Percentage of recorded accidents involving at least one fatal injury within the selected filter context. | `Fatal Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `injuries_fatal`, `fact_id` | A fatal accident is identified when `injuries_fatal > 0`. `Total Accidents` follows KPI-01 and includes all recorded accident records in the selected filter context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-04** | **Percentage Increase** | BQ1 | Percentage increase in severe accident rate under predefined adverse environmental conditions compared with predefined baseline environmental conditions. | `((Scenario Rate − Baseline Rate) / Baseline Rate) × 100` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `is_severe` | **Baseline conditions:** Weather = `CLEAR` or `CLOUDY/OVERCAST`; Lighting = `DAYLIGHT` or `DARKNESS, LIGHTED ROAD`.<br><br>**Adverse conditions:** Weather = `RAIN`, `SNOW`, `BLOWING SNOW`, `FREEZING RAIN/DRIZZLE`, `FOG/SMOKE/HAZE`, `SLEET/HAIL`, `SEVERE CROSS WIND GATE`, or `BLOWING SAND, SOIL, DIRT`; Lighting = `DARKNESS`.<br><br>The comparison is based on Severe Rate under each predefined condition set. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-05** | **Risk Score (EPDO Score)** | BQ2 | Weighted accident severity score calculated from the number of persons in each injury-severity category and a no-injury accident component.<br><br>**Grain:** Accident record. | `EPDO = (12 × N_fatal) + (3 × N_incapacitating) + (3 × N_non-incapacitating) + (3 × N_reported-not-evident) + N_no-injury` | `injuries_fatal`, `injuries_incapacitating`, `injuries_non_incapacitating`, `injuries_reported_not_evident`, `injuries_total` | **Weights:** Fatal injury = 12; incapacitating injury = 3; non-incapacitating injury = 3; reported-not-evident injury = 3; no recorded injury = 1.<br><br>`N_fatal`, `N_incapacitating`, `N_non-incapacitating`, and `N_reported-not-evident` represent the number of persons in each injury category. `N_no-injury` represents an accident record where `injuries_total = 0`.<br><br>Higher EPDO scores indicate greater accident severity. Methodology adapted from the referenced EPDO methodology. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-06** | **Severe Accidents under Favorable Conditions** | BQ3 | Total number of severe accidents occurring under predefined favorable environmental conditions. | `COUNTROWS(is_severe = TRUE)` under favorable conditions | `is_severe`, `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | **Favorable environmental conditions:** Weather = `CLEAR` or `CLOUDY/OVERCAST`; Lighting = `DAYLIGHT` or `DARKNESS, LIGHTED ROAD`.<br><br>Each severe accident record is counted once. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-07** | **Accident Count by Cause** | BQ3 | Number of recorded accidents grouped by primary contributing cause within the selected filter context. | `COUNT(fact_id) GROUP BY prim_contributory_cause` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `fact_id` | Each accident record is counted once. Values such as `UNABLE TO DETERMINE`, `NOT REPORTED`, or other insufficient-information categories are retained as separate categories unless explicitly excluded by the analytical scope. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-08** | **Cause Distribution (%)** | BQ3 | Percentage contribution of each contributing-cause category to severe accidents occurring under favorable environmental conditions. | `Severe Accidents by Cause / Total Severe Accidents under Favorable Conditions × 100` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `is_severe`, `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | The numerator includes severe accidents under favorable environmental conditions for each cause category. The denominator is the total number of severe accidents under the same favorable environmental conditions. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-10** | **Severe Total** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ3, BQ4 | Total number of recorded accident records classified as severe within the selected filter context. | `COUNTROWS(is_severe = TRUE)` | `is_severe`, `fact_id` | A severe accident is an accident with at least one fatal or incapacitating injury. Count each severe accident record once. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-11** | **Total Fatal Injuries** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Total number of fatal injuries recorded across the selected accident records. | `SUM(injuries_fatal)` | `injuries_fatal` | Each recorded fatal injury contributes one count. This metric measures **fatal injuries**, not the number of fatal accident records. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-12** | **Total EPDO Score** | BQ2, BQ4 | Total weighted accident severity score across the selected accident records based on the standardized EPDO methodology. | `SUM(EPDO Score)` | `injuries_fatal`, `injuries_incapacitating`, `injuries_non_incapacitating`, `injuries_reported_not_evident`, `injuries_total` | EPDO is calculated using the weighting methodology defined in KPI-05. The total is aggregated within the selected filter context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-13** | **Average EPDO Score** | BQ2, BQ4 | Average EPDO severity score per recorded accident within the selected filter context. | `Total EPDO Score / Total Accident Records` | `fact_id`, `injuries_fatal`, `injuries_incapacitating`, `injuries_non_incapacitating`, `injuries_reported_not_evident`, `injuries_total` | Calculate the average EPDO score per recorded accident. The denominator follows KPI-01 and therefore includes all recorded accident records within the selected filter context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-14** | **Priority Trafficway Count** | BQ4 | Number of trafficway types that have both above-average accident volume and above-average average EPDO score within the selected analytical context. | `COUNT(Trafficway Types where Total Accidents > Average Accident Count AND Average EPDO Score > Average EPDO Baseline)` | `trafficway_type`, `fact_id`, `injuries_fatal`, `injuries_incapacitating`, `injuries_non_incapacitating`, `injuries_reported_not_evident`, `injuries_total` | A trafficway type is classified as a priority trafficway only when **both** its accident count and average EPDO score exceed their respective dynamically calculated baselines.<br><br>**Average Accident Count:** average number of accidents per trafficway type within the selected filter context.<br><br>**Average EPDO Baseline:** average EPDO score across the analyzed trafficway types within the selected filter context.<br><br>Priority classification is relative to the selected analytical context and does not represent an absolute "critical" threshold. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-15** | **Human Error Ratio** | BQ3 | Percentage of recorded accidents primarily associated with human-factor causes within the selected filter context. | `Human Factor Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `cause_group`, `fact_id` | Human-factor accidents are identified through the standardized `cause_group = "Human Factors"` classification in `dim_cause`.<br><br>The numerator includes accident records classified as `Human Factors`; the denominator follows KPI-01.<br><br>Unknown or insufficient-information causes remain outside the Human Factors group unless explicitly classified as such in the cause mapping. | Data Analytics Team |
