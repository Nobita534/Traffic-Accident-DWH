# Metric Dictionary

## 1. Document Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the business metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used in the Traffic Safety Analytics Platform. It provides standardized metric definitions, calculation logic, business rules, and required data to ensure consistency across analytical models, dashboards, and reports.

---

## 2. Metric Dictionary

# KPI Dictionary

| **Metric ID** | **Metric Name** | **Business Question** | **Definition** | **Formula** | **Required Data** | **Business Rules** | **Owner** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KPI-01** | **Total Accidents** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Total number of recorded traffic accident records within the selected filter context.<br><br>**Grain:** One fact record represents one recorded accident. | `COUNT(fact_id)` | `fact_id` | Count each accident record once using the unique `fact_id`. The denominator includes all recorded accident records, including records containing unknown, not reported, or unable-to-determine information. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-02** | **Severe Rate** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Percentage of recorded accidents classified as severe within the selected filter context. | `Severe Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `is_severe`, `fact_id` | A severe accident is defined as an accident with at least one fatal injury or incapacitating injury. `Total Accidents` follows KPI-01 and includes all recorded accident records in the selected filter context. For BQ2, this is a severity proportion among recorded crashes, not a crash-occurrence probability. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-03** | **Fatal Rate** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Percentage of recorded accidents involving at least one fatal injury within the selected filter context. | `Fatal Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `injuries_fatal`, `fact_id` | A fatal accident is identified when `injuries_fatal > 0`. `Total Accidents` follows KPI-01 and includes all recorded accident records in the selected filter context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-04** | **Percentage Increase** | BQ1 | Percentage increase in severe accident rate under predefined adverse environmental conditions compared with predefined baseline environmental conditions. | `((Scenario Rate − Baseline Rate) / Baseline Rate) × 100` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `is_severe` | **Baseline conditions:** Weather = `CLEAR` or `CLOUDY/OVERCAST`; Lighting = `DAYLIGHT` or `DARKNESS, LIGHTED ROAD`.<br><br>**Adverse conditions:** Weather = `RAIN`, `SNOW`, `BLOWING SNOW`, `FREEZING RAIN/DRIZZLE`, `FOG/SMOKE/HAZE`, `SLEET/HAIL`, `SEVERE CROSS WIND GATE`, or `BLOWING SAND, SOIL, DIRT`; Lighting = `DARKNESS`.<br><br>The comparison is based on Severe Rate under each predefined condition set. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-05** | **Crash Severity Weight** | BQ4 | Crash-level severity weight used as the numerator component of the 12-3-1 Severity Index.<br><br>**Grain:** Accident record. | `Fatal crash = 12; non-fatal injury crash = 3; PDO/no-injury crash = 1` | `injuries_fatal`, `injuries_total` | Classification is mutually exclusive at crash grain. A crash with `injuries_fatal > 0` receives 12. Otherwise, a crash with `injuries_total > 0` receives 3. All remaining crashes receive 1. The weighting follows the 12-3-1 crash severity index approach documented in an FHWA case study. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-06** | **Severe Accidents under Favorable Conditions** | BQ3 | Total number of severe accidents occurring under predefined favorable environmental conditions. | `COUNTROWS(is_severe = TRUE)` under favorable conditions | `is_severe`, `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | **Favorable environmental conditions:** Weather = `CLEAR` or `CLOUDY/OVERCAST`; Lighting = `DAYLIGHT` or `DARKNESS, LIGHTED ROAD`.<br><br>Each severe accident record is counted once. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-07** | **Accident Count by Cause** | BQ3 | Number of recorded accidents grouped by primary contributing cause within the selected filter context. | `COUNT(fact_id) GROUP BY prim_contributory_cause` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `fact_id` | Each accident record is counted once. Values such as `UNABLE TO DETERMINE`, `NOT REPORTED`, or other insufficient-information categories are retained as separate categories unless explicitly excluded by the analytical scope. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-08** | **Cause Distribution (%)** | BQ3 | Percentage contribution of each contributing-cause category to severe accidents occurring under favorable environmental conditions. | `Severe Accidents by Cause / Total Severe Accidents under Favorable Conditions × 100` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `is_severe`, `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | The numerator includes severe accidents under favorable environmental conditions for each cause category. The denominator is the total number of severe accidents under the same favorable environmental conditions. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-10** | **Severe Total** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ3, BQ4 | Total number of recorded accident records classified as severe within the selected filter context. | `COUNTROWS(is_severe = TRUE)` | `is_severe`, `fact_id` | A severe accident is an accident with at least one fatal or incapacitating injury. Count each severe accident record once. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-11** | **Total Fatal Injuries** | BQ1, BQ2, BQ4 | Total number of fatal injuries recorded across the selected accident records. | `SUM(injuries_fatal)` | `injuries_fatal` | Each recorded fatal injury contributes one count. This metric measures **fatal injuries**, not the number of fatal accident records. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-12** | **Total Severity Weight** | BQ4 | Sum of crash-level 12-3-1 severity weights across the selected accident records. | `SUM(crash_severity_weight)` | `crash_severity_weight` | Each accident contributes exactly one mutually exclusive severity weight defined in KPI-05. This is an intermediate aggregate used to calculate Severity Index. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-13** | **Severity Index** | BQ4 | Weighted average crash severity within the selected filter context using the 12-3-1 crash-level weighting scheme. | `(12F + 3I + 1PDO) / N`, equivalent to `Total Severity Weight / Total Accidents` | `crash_severity_weight`, `fact_id` | `F` = fatal crashes, `I` = non-fatal injury crashes, `PDO` = property-damage-only/no-injury crashes, and `N` = total crashes. The calculation counts crashes, not persons injured or killed. Higher values indicate a more severe crash mix within the selected context. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-14** | **Priority Trafficway Count** | BQ4 | Number of trafficway types that have both above-average accident volume and above-average Severity Index within the selected analytical context. | `COUNT(Trafficway Types where Total Accidents > Average Accident Count AND Severity Index > Average Severity Index Baseline)` | `trafficway_type`, `fact_id`, `crash_severity_weight` | A trafficway type is classified as a priority trafficway only when **both** its accident count and Severity Index exceed their respective dynamically calculated baselines.<br><br>**Average Accident Count:** average number of accidents per trafficway type within the selected filter context.<br><br>**Average Severity Index Baseline:** average Severity Index across analyzed trafficway types within the selected filter context.<br><br>Priority classification is relative to the selected analytical context and does not represent an absolute "critical" threshold. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-15** | **Human Error Ratio** | BQ3 | Percentage of recorded accidents primarily associated with human-factor causes within the selected filter context. | `Human Factor Accidents / Total Accidents × 100` | `prim_contributory_cause`, `cause_group`, `fact_id` | Human-factor accidents are identified through the standardized `cause_group = "Human Factors"` classification in `dim_cause`.<br><br>The numerator includes accident records classified as `Human Factors`; the denominator follows KPI-01.<br><br>Unknown or insufficient-information causes remain outside the Human Factors group unless explicitly classified as such in the cause mapping. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-16** | **Accident Volume P25** | BQ2 | 25th percentile of `Total Accidents` across visible `weather_condition × lighting_condition` combinations with at least one recorded crash. | `PERCENTILEX.INC(condition groups, Total Accident, 0.25)` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | Calculated dynamically in the selected report context. Used as the lower boundary for BQ2 volume-support classification. Groups at or below P25 are classified as Low Volume. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-17** | **Accident Volume P75** | BQ2 | 75th percentile of `Total Accidents` across visible `weather_condition × lighting_condition` combinations with at least one recorded crash. | `PERCENTILEX.INC(condition groups, Total Accident, 0.75)` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | Calculated dynamically in the selected report context. High Volume groups satisfy `Total Accident >= P75`. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-18** | **Accident Volume Bucket** | BQ2 | Support classification for each environmental combination based on its recorded-crash count relative to P25 and P75. | `Low: N <= P25; Medium: P25 < N < P75; High: N >= P75` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id` | All groups remain visible in the matrix. `Peak Severe Condition` ranking is restricted to High Volume groups. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-19** | **Average Units per Accident** | BQ2 | Average number of vehicles or units involved per recorded accident within the selected environmental combination. | `SUM(num_units) / Total Accidents` | `num_units`, `fact_id` | Supporting context only. This metric does not represent traffic exposure and must not be interpreted as accident risk. | Data Analytics Team |
| **KPI-20** | **Peak Severe Condition** | BQ2 | Environmental combination with the highest Severe Rate among High Volume groups. | `TOP 1 weather × lighting group with Total Accident >= P75 by Severe Rate` | `weather_condition`, `lighting_condition`, `fact_id`, `is_severe` | Eligibility requires `Total Accident >= P75`. Rank eligible groups by Severe Rate; ties are resolved by higher Total Accident and then deterministic category ordering. | Data Analytics Team |

> **BQ2 limitation:** The dataset does not contain a traffic-exposure denominator such as traffic volume, trips, or vehicle-miles traveled. Therefore, BQ2 metrics describe severity among recorded crashes and should not be interpreted as the probability of a crash occurring.

### Severity Index Reference

The 12-3-1 crash-level weighting used by KPI-05 and KPI-13 follows the FHWA case-study severity-index equation: `(12F + 3I + 1PDO) / N`, where `F`, `I`, and `PDO` are counts of fatal, injury, and property-damage-only crashes respectively.
