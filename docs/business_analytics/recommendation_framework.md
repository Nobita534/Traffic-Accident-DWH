# Recommendation Framework

## 1. Overview

The Recommendation Framework is based on the findings from the Root Cause Analysis. It provides practical recommendations to improve road infrastructure, traffic facilities, and accident prevention under different environmental conditions and human-related factors.

---

# 2. Recommendation Framework

## 2.1 Business Question 1

### Business Question

How much does the severe accident rate increase under bad weather and low-light conditions compared to normal conditions?

### Root Cause

Severe accidents occur more frequently when bad weather and poor road surface conditions happen at the same time. These conditions reduce vehicle control and increase the risk of serious crashes.

### Recommendation

- Improve road lighting in areas where accidents frequently occur during bad weather.
- Increase road maintenance for damaged road surfaces and areas with sand or loose materials.
- Install warning signs or electronic message boards in high-risk locations during adverse weather conditions.

### Expected Impact

- Reduce accident risk in areas with poor environmental conditions.
- Help drivers recognize potential hazards earlier.

### Stakeholder

- Road Safety Analysts
- Transportation Authorities

---

## 2.2 Business Question 2

### Business Question

Which combination of environmental factors creates the highest accident risk?

### Root Cause

Multiple adverse environmental conditions can reduce visibility, vehicle control, and driver reaction time at the same time.

### Recommendation

- Create a list of high-risk environmental combinations for safety monitoring.
- Deploy real-time warning systems in areas where dangerous environmental conditions often occur.
- Regularly monitor environmental combinations with high Severe Rate to support road safety planning.

### Expected Impact

- Help identify high-risk conditions earlier.
- Support better accident prevention planning.

### Stakeholder

- Road Safety Analysts
- Transportation Authorities

---

## 2.3 Business Question 3

### Business Question

Which driver behaviors remain the main causes of severe accidents under favorable environmental conditions?

### Root Cause

Human behavior remains the main cause of traffic accidents. In addition, the high number of **UNABLE TO DETERMINE** records indicates that accident causes are still incomplete or not fully recorded.

### Recommendation

- Promote road safety campaigns to encourage compliance with traffic rules and right-of-way regulations.
- Increase monitoring in areas with a high rate of traffic violations.
- Standardize the accident reporting process to reduce the number of **UNABLE TO DETERMINE** records in future datasets.

### Expected Impact

- Improve data quality for future analysis.
- Support more accurate identification of accident causes.

### Stakeholder

- Road Safety Analysts
- Policy Makers

---

## 2.4 Business Question 4

### Business Question

Which locations or traffic scenarios should be given priority for investment to reduce severe accidents?

### Root Cause

The **NOT DIVIDED** trafficway type has both a high number of accidents and a higher-than-average accident severity level, making it the highest-priority area for road safety improvement.

### Recommendation

- Prioritize infrastructure assessment and improvement for **NOT DIVIDED** roads.
- Increase warning signs, road markings, and other safety measures in high-accident locations.
- Use both **Total Accident** and **Avg EPDO Score** to determine improvement priorities instead of relying only on accident counts.

### Expected Impact

- Support better allocation of road safety resources.
- Improve the effectiveness of infrastructure improvement projects.

### Stakeholder

- Transportation Authorities
- Road Safety Analysts

# 3. Summary

| Category | Recommendation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Infrastructure Safety** | Prioritize infrastructure improvements and warning systems in high-risk locations, especially **NOT DIVIDED** roads. |
| **Environmental Risk Management** | Monitor environmental combinations with high Severe Rate and implement suitable warning measures. |
| **Human Behavior Improvement** | Strengthen traffic monitoring and improve driver awareness to reduce human-related accidents. |
| **Data Quality Enhancement** | Standardize accident reporting to reduce **UNABLE TO DETERMINE** records and improve data quality for future analysis. |
